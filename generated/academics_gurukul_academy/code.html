Curriculum Overview

Gurukul Academy strictly follows the national curriculum prescribed by the **Curriculum Development Centre (CDC)** and the **National Examination Board (NEB)** of Nepal. Our approach enhances this core with international best practices to ensure global competency.

NEB Alignment

Fully compliant with Secondary Education Examination (SEE) and Grade 12 board standards.

CDC Framework

Core subjects integrated with skill-based learning and local cultural context.
